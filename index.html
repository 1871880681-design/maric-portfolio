Work Details

Close readings of composition, mood, and visual expression.

01

Hello Future

Hello Future presents a cool-toned experimental portrait poster with a digital and futuristic atmosphere. The blue negative-like treatment, strong facial contrast, and scattered visual noise create a sense of distance, technology, and uncertainty. The work focuses on mood, texture, and visual tension, using a restrained composition to make the portrait feel both mysterious and contemporary.

02

Blooming Whisper

Blooming Whisper combines a soft illustrated portrait with floral and butterfly elements to create a gentle, poetic visual mood. The composition feels light and expressive, using organic shapes to frame the character and suggest growth, emotion, and quiet imagination. Its delicate style gives the poster a calm yet memorable personality, suitable for a more artistic visual narrative.

03

Porcelain Fusion

Porcelain Fusion explores the collision between traditional ceramic patterns and contemporary poster composition. Blue-and-white porcelain textures are combined with colorful decorative fragments, creating a layered image that feels both cultural and modern. The work highlights contrast, rhythm, and visual reconstruction, transforming familiar traditional symbols into a fresh graphic language.

04

La Renaissance

La Renaissance uses bold color blocks, strong typography, and expressive composition to reinterpret the idea of renaissance through a modern graphic lens. The poster creates visual impact through contrast and arrangement, balancing classical reference with contemporary energy. Its structure feels experimental but controlled, showing an interest in type, color, and cultural visual expression.

05

Visual Identity Wall

Visual Identity Wall works like a graphic design statement board, combining signage, typography, image treatment, and layered visual elements. The composition suggests a study of visual identity, communication, and environmental graphics. Its collage-like structure gives the work an energetic academic quality, presenting design as a system of signs, directions, and visual relationships.

06

Global Education News Scroll

Global Education News Scroll is a long-format information design work with an editorial reading experience. The layout organizes news-style content, images, and text blocks into a vertical visual flow. It emphasizes hierarchy, readability, and structured communication, making the long image suitable for a scroll-based display. The work reflects an ability to manage information visually across an extended format.
